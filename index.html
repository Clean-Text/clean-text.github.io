Clean Text

Clean Text ensures your pasted content adapts seamlessly to any document or app. It strips away unwanted formatting—colors, fonts, styles—while handling messy spaces, line breaks, and more. Perfect for precise text handling on macOS.

Fix Paragraphs

Removes returns within paragraphs and eliminates multiple empty lines between them for clean, readable structure.

Normalize Spaces and Tabs

Replaces multiple spaces with singles, trims leading and trailing spaces, and converts tabs to precise spacing—or vice versa.

Smart Quotes and Case

Converts between straight and curly quotes, capitalizes sentences or words, shifts to upper/lowercase, or applies random case with control.

Advanced Cleanup

Removes duplicates, sorts lines, handles encodings (Mac to Windows and back), ROT-13, reversals, ligatures, and line ending conversions.

Effortless Printing

Prepares text for ink-efficient prints, avoiding browser formatting waste.

How It Works
